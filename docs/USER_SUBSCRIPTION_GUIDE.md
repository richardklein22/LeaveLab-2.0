## Overview

- **Priority support**: Get help when you need it

## Getting Started

### Signing Up

1. **Create an account** at [LeaveLab](https://leavelab.com/signup)
   - Use email/password or Google OAuth
   - Verify your email address

2. **Choose your plan** at [Pricing Page](https://leavelab.com/pricing)
   - Start with Free to explore
   - Upgrade anytime to Basic or Premium

3. **For paid plans**: Complete checkout via Stripe
   - Secure payment processing
   - No credit card for Free tier
   - Premium tier: Optional 7-day free trial

### Selecting a Plan

**Monthly vs Annual**:

| Billing Cycle | Basic | Premium | Savings |
|---------------|-------|---------|---------|
| **Monthly** | £70/month | £100/month | - |
| **Annual** | £672/year (£56/month) | £960/year (£80/month) | **20% off** |

💡 **Pro Tip**: Choose annual billing to save money and commit to your long-term goals. Big life changes take time!

### Checkout Process

1. Click "Subscribe" on your chosen tier
2. You'll be redirected to Stripe Checkout (secure payment page)
3. Enter your payment details:
   - Card number
   - Expiry date
   - CVC code
   - Billing address
4. Click "Subscribe"
5. You'll be redirected back to LeaveLab
6. Your subscription is now active! 🎉

**Payment Security**: We use Stripe for payment processing. Your card details are never stored on our servers.

## Managing Your Subscription

### Viewing Your Subscription

1. Log in to your LeaveLab account
2. Go to **Dashboard** → **Subscription**
3. You'll see:
   - Current tier
   - Billing cycle (monthly/annual)
   - Next billing date
   - Billing amount
   - Payment method (last 4 digits)

### Upgrading Your Plan

**From Free to Basic/Premium**:
1. Go to [Pricing Page](https://leavelab.com/pricing)
2. Click "Subscribe" on your desired tier
3. Complete checkout

**From Basic to Premium**:
1. Go to [Pricing Page](https://leavelab.com/pricing)
2. Click "Upgrade to Premium"
3. You'll be charged a prorated amount
4. Your existing billing cycle continues

💡 **Pro Tip**: Upgrades are prorated - you only pay for the time remaining in your billing cycle.

### Downgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Subscription**
2. Click "Manage Subscription" (opens Stripe Customer Portal)
3. Click "Update plan"
4. Select your new tier
5. Confirm

**Important**: 
- Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period
- You keep access to your current tier until the period ends
- Example: If you downgrade on January 15th and your billing renews February 1st, you'll have Premium access until February 1st

### Cancelling Your Subscription

**From Paid to Free**:

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Subscription**
2. Click "Manage Subscription" (opens Stripe Customer Portal)
3. Click "Cancel subscription"
4. Confirm cancellation
5. You'll receive a confirmation email

**What Happens**:
- ✅ You keep access until the end of your current billing period
- ✅ No further charges
- ✅ You'll be moved to the Free tier automatically
- ✅ Your account and progress are saved
- ✅ You can reactivate anytime

**Changed Your Mind?**:
- You can reactivate your subscription before the period ends
- Go to the Customer Portal and click "Reactivate"

## Billing & Payments

### Payment Methods

We accept:
- ✅ Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- ✅ Debit cards
- ✅ Apple Pay / Google Pay
- ❌ PayPal (coming soon)
- ❌ Bank transfers (coming soon)

### Billing Cycle

**Monthly**:
- Charged every month on the same date you subscribed
- Example: Subscribe on January 15th → charged on 15th of each month

**Annual**:
- Charged once per year
- Save 20% compared to monthly billing
- Best value for committed learners

### Updating Payment Method

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Subscription**
2. Click "Manage Subscription" (opens Stripe Customer Portal)
3. Click "Update payment method"
4. Enter new card details
5. Click "Save"

Your next payment will use the new method.

### Failed Payments

If your payment fails:

1. **Day 0**: Payment attempt fails
   - You'll receive an email notification
   - Stripe will retry in 3 days

2. **Day 3**: Second retry
   - Another email if it fails again
   - Stripe will retry in 5 days

3. **Day 8**: Third retry
   - Final email if it fails
   - Stripe will retry in 7 days

4. **Day 15**: Final attempt
   - If payment still fails, your subscription is cancelled
   - You're moved to the Free tier
   - All progress is saved

**To Avoid Issues**:
- ✅ Keep your payment method up to date
- ✅ Ensure sufficient funds are available
- ✅ Check your card hasn't expired
- ✅ Update your billing address if you move

### Invoices & Receipts

**Accessing Invoices**:
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Subscription**
2. Click "Manage Subscription"
3. Click "Billing history"
4. Download any invoice as PDF

**Email Receipts**:
- You'll automatically receive an email receipt after each payment
- Check your spam folder if you don't see it

### Refunds

**Refund Policy**:
- ❌ **Monthly subscriptions**: No refunds (cancel anytime, no future charges)
- ❌ **Annual subscriptions**: No refunds for unused months
- ✅ **Trial period**: Cancel during trial for no charge
- ✅ **Exceptional cases**: Contact support@leavelab.com

**Why No Refunds?**:
- You have immediate access to all content
- We offer a 7-day trial for Premium (test before committing)
- You can cancel anytime to avoid future charges

**EU/UK Consumer Rights**:
- You waive your 14-day cooling-off period when accessing digital content
- This is standard for digital subscriptions

## Troubleshooting

### "Payment Declined"

**Solutions**:
1. Check card details are correct
2. Ensure sufficient funds
3. Contact your bank (they may have blocked the transaction)
4. Try a different card
5. Try a different payment method (Apple Pay, Google Pay)

**Still Not Working?**:
- Email support@leavelab.com with:
  - Your email address
  - Error message screenshot
  - Last 4 digits of card

---

### "Can't Access Premium Content"

**Check These**:
1. **Subscription status**: Go to Dashboard → Subscription
   - Is your subscription active?
   - Has your payment failed?

2. **Tier level**: What tier are you on?
   - Free: Only first lessons
   - Basic: 1 course only
   - Premium: All content

3. **Course selection** (Basic tier only):
   - Have you selected your 1 allowed course?
   - Go to Courses → Select your course

4. **Browser cache**:
   - Hard refresh the page (Cmd+Shift+R / Ctrl+Shift+R)
   - Clear browser cache
   - Try incognito/private mode

**Still Not Working?**:
- Email support@leavelab.com

---

### "Charged Twice"

**This can happen if**:
- You have two accounts with different emails
- You upgraded mid-cycle (prorated charge)
- Your billing date is today (looks like duplicate)

**Solutions**:
1. Check your Stripe Customer Portal for billing history
2. Check both charges are the same amount
3. If different amounts: One is a prorated upgrade
4. If duplicate: Email support@leavelab.com immediately

---

### "Trial Didn't Cancel"

**What happened**:
- You were charged because the trial auto-converted to paid
- The trial period ended (7 days)

**Solutions**:
1. Cancel immediately to prevent future charges
2. Email support@leavelab.com if you believe this was an error
3. We may offer a refund on a case-by-case basis

**To Avoid This**:
- ✅ Set a calendar reminder for Day 5 of your trial
- ✅ Cancel during the trial if you're unsure
- ✅ You can always resubscribe later

---

### "Can't Find Customer Portal"

**Location**:
1. Log in to LeaveLab
2. Go to **Dashboard**
3. Click **Subscription** tab
4. Click **"Manage Subscription"** button
5. This opens the Stripe Customer Portal

**What You Can Do There**:
- Update payment method
- Change billing cycle
- Upgrade/downgrade tier
- Cancel subscription
- View billing history
- Download invoices

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?**  
A: Yes! Go to the Customer Portal → Update plan → Choose your billing cycle. If switching from monthly to annual, you'll be charged a prorated amount. If switching from annual to monthly, the change takes effect at the end of your annual period.

**Q: Do you offer student discounts?**  
A: Not yet, but we're considering it! Email support@leavelab.com to express interest.

**Q: Can I share my account with someone?**  
A: No, accounts are for individual use only. Sharing violates our Terms of Service and may result in account suspension.

**Q: What happens if I travel to a different country?**  
A: Nothing! Your subscription works worldwide. Prices remain in GBP regardless of your location.

**Q: Do you offer team/group plans?**  
A: Not yet, but they're on our roadmap. Email support@leavelab.com to express interest.

---

### Content Access

**Q: How do I choose my 1 course on the Basic tier?**  
A: Go to Courses → Browse → Click on any course → Click "Select as my Basic course". You can change this once per month.

**Q: Can I download courses for offline viewing?**  
A: Not yet, but this feature is planned for 2025.

**Q: What's the difference between short-term and long-term visa info?**  
A: 
- **Short-term**: Tourist visas, visa waivers, stays up to 90 days
- **Long-term**: Digital nomad visas, work permits, residency, 6+ months

---

### Billing Questions

**Q: Why do I see charges from "Stripe" not "LeaveLab"?**  
A: Stripe is our payment processor. All charges come from Stripe on behalf of LeaveLab. This is normal and secure.

**Q: Can I get an invoice for my company?**  
A: Yes! In the Customer Portal → Billing History → Download Invoice. You can also update your billing details to include company name and VAT number.

**Q: Will prices increase?**  
A: If you're on an active subscription, your price is locked in. Price increases only apply to new subscribers or when you cancel and resubscribe.

**Q: Do you charge VAT/tax?**  
A: Currently, prices are shown as charged (no additional VAT added). This may change based on your location and tax regulations.

---

### Account Management

**Q: What happens if I delete my account?**  
A: Your account is soft-deleted for 30 days (you can recover it). Your subscription is automatically cancelled. After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted.

**Q: Can I reactivate a cancelled subscription?**  
A: Yes! If you're still within your paid period, you can reactivate in the Customer Portal. If your period has ended, just subscribe again on the pricing page.

**Q: How do I export my data?**  
A: Go to Account Settings → Data & Privacy → Export Data. You'll receive a JSON file with all your account data (GDPR compliant).
